
## 2024-05-18 - Associate form labels with their corresponding inputs
**Learning:** Found a pattern of missing explicit `htmlFor` and `id` associations in forms (specifically in the Account Details form), hindering screen reader accessibility and decreasing the clickable area for form fields.
**Action:** Moving forward, ensure all visual labels are explicitly associated with their respective inputs using matching `htmlFor` and `id` attributes. This should be a standard check for any forms in the application to guarantee full accessibility.
