## 2024-05-15 - Contact Form Button Feedback\n**Learning:** Users submitting a contact form might lack immediate visual feedback, causing them to click submit multiple times or feel uncertain if their message was sent successfully. Adding a simple loading/success state to the submit button greatly improves user confidence.\n**Action:** Implemented inline feedback on the contact form submit button by changing its text/state to "Sending..." and then "Message Sent!" rather than relying on global toasts, which can be easily missed.
